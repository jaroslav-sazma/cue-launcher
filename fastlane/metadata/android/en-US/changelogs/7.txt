The Mac bridge: pair the phone with Cue: Relay & Reply, the free companion app on your Mac. Notifications you choose appear on the Mac, where you can reply to or dismiss them. Send selected text or your clipboard to the Mac, fetch the Mac's clipboard, and use the new scenario pieces: the conditions "Mac nearby" and "Mac locked", and the action "Lock the Mac". Everything is end-to-end encrypted between your own devices; no server, no account.
